Reliable RF localisation for capsule endoscopy remains challenging because of complex in-body propagation and measurement uncertainty. In this work, we explore an anchor-aware conditional flow-matching approach that improves localisation robustness while staying consistent with the anchor geometry and the measurements.

*This research was carried out at the Antennas and Electromagnetics Research Group and CE-Track, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London.*
