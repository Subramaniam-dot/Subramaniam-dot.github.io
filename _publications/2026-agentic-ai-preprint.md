Agentic AI systems are increasingly viewed as a viable response to the shortcomings of static, rigid and human-in-the-loop artificial intelligence (AI) systems, because autonomous operation enables rapid adaptation to dynamic, complex problems with improved time-critical behaviour under real-world constraints.

Despite significant progress, current agentic pipelines are still challenged by output instability, scalability gaps and system-integration issues. To address these limitations, this article presents a conceptual framework that unifies core AI functionality with implementation approaches across different system scales. It also surveys popular applications of agentic AI and sets out open problems and directions for future work.
