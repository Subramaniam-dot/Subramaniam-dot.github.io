I am a PhD student in Electronic Engineering at Queen Mary University of London, funded by the EPSRC DTP. My research focuses on developing AI-driven RF localisation for wireless capsule endoscopy using SDR-based measurements and wearable multi-antenna systems.

## Research Interests

- RF Signal Detection and Localisation
- Software-Defined Radio (SDR)
- Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Wireless Capsule Endoscopy
- Wearable Antenna Systems

## Technical Skills

- **Programming:** Python (Django, Flask), SQL
- **Machine Learning:** TensorFlow, PyTorch
- **Cloud:** AWS (Lambda, EC2, RDS)
- **Hardware:** Software-Defined Radio, Antenna Design
