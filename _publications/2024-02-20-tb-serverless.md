We propose a neural-network-based smart e-health application for the prediction of tuberculosis (TB) using serverless computing. DenseNet-201, VGG-19 and MobileNet-V3-Small architectures are trained, validated and compared using transfer learning. The best-performing model, VGG-19, is deployed in both server-based and serverless environments, and its performance is measured with JMeter.
