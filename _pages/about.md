## Research

My doctoral research is on **machine learning for radio-frequency (RF) localisation in wireless capsule endoscopy (WCE)**. Capsule endoscopy offers minimally invasive imaging of the gastrointestinal tract, but locating the capsule inside the body remains an open problem: in-body radio propagation is highly heterogeneous, frequency-dependent and difficult to model in closed form.

My work combines **software-defined radio (SDR)** measurement with **wearable multi-antenna arrays** and **probabilistic machine learning** to estimate capsule position from on-body RF observations. Recent work introduces an *anchor-aware conditional flow-matching* model that improves localisation robustness while staying consistent with the antenna geometry and the measured signals ([EuCAP 2026](/publication/2026-eucap-rf-localization)).

The work draws on antenna engineering, statistical signal processing and machine learning, with applications in medical devices and digital health. I have also contributed to research on machine learning for e-health and on cloud and edge computing.
